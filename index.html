Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026Last updated: November 3, 2026Developer: nasrouDevPackage: com.medgnosia.app

Privacy summary: Based on the technical audit supplied for this policy, Medgnosia stores the described profile, study, preference, and assistant data locally on your device. The audited version does not send this information to a Medgnosia server or an external analytics, advertising, or AI service. This must be rechecked whenever the app changes.

1. Introduction

Medgnosia (“Medgnosia,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is an Android educational application for medical and other healthcare students. It provides medical question banks, flashcards, study progress features, exam scheduling, and an in-app study assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Medgnosia app (package name: com.medgnosia.app).

Medgnosia is designed to work offline. Based on the application audit supplied for this policy, the app stores the information described below on your device and does not send it to a Medgnosia-operated server or an external analytics, advertising, or AI service. This statement must be reviewed whenever the app’s code, dependencies, or services change.

2. Information You Enter or Create

Depending on the features you use, information may be created or stored on your device, including:

  • Optional profile details, such as a display name, professional role, specialty, and profile picture.
  • Study content you create or import, such as flashcards, decks, chapters, question activity, flagged items, and exam schedule titles and dates.
  • Learning activity and progress, such as answers, scores, review ratings, study time, and streak information.
  • App preferences, such as language, theme, study goals, notification preferences, and flashcard scheduling settings.
  • Text you enter into the in-app study assistant. The supplied technical audit describes the assistant as using locally implemented, rule-based logic; it does not describe a remote generative-AI service.

Please do not enter identifiable patient information, medical records, or other confidential information about another person into Medgnosia, including in flashcards, questions, or assistant prompts.

3. How and Where Information Is Stored

According to the supplied technical audit, the information above is stored locally in Android app storage, including the app’s Room database, DataStore preferences, and private files. Profile pictures and flashcard media are stored as local files. Study-assistant conversation state is held in the app’s memory during use.

The app does not provide a Medgnosia account, remote profile, or cloud-sync service according to that audit. Your locally stored information is therefore not available to us through a Medgnosia server.

4. Study Assistant

The supplied audit describes the study assistant as locally implemented, rule-based functionality. It reports that prompts and conversation state are processed on the device and are not sent to an external AI provider. The assistant should not be treated as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

5. Sharing, Exporting, and Third-Party Services

The supplied audit did not identify Firebase, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in the inspected project. It also reported no transmission of user-entered study or profile information to external servers. This description applies only to the audited app version and must be updated if third-party SDKs or network services are added.

If you choose to export flashcards or share content, Medgnosia creates or passes the selected file/content to the Android sharing mechanism and the destination app or service you choose. The destination’s handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

6. Permissions and Device Features

The supplied audit identifies the following permissions or device features:

  • Camera: used when you explicitly choose to take a profile picture. You can decline permission and use an available alternative, such as selecting an image, if offered by your device/app version.
  • Notifications: used for study reminders and exam notifications. You can manage notification permission and settings through Android.
  • Exact alarms and device-restart handling: used to schedule or restore reminders and exam notifications.

The exact permissions and available alternatives may vary by app version and Android version. You can review or change permissions in your device’s Android settings.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall Medgnosia, subject to Android’s device and backup behavior. You can delete individual content through the app where the relevant delete controls are available.

  • To remove all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Medgnosia → Storage → Clear data (the exact labels may vary by device).
  • Uninstalling the app ordinarily removes its app-private local data. Any files you exported or shared to another location or app must be deleted separately from that location.

Because the audited version does not use Medgnosia accounts or a Medgnosia server, there is no server-side account or profile for us to delete.

8. Security

Medgnosia relies on Android’s app sandbox and platform security features to protect app-private files. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep Android and the app up to date.

9. Children’s Privacy

Medgnosia is intended as an educational tool for people studying medicine and related healthcare subjects. It is not designed or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the details below.

10. Medical and Patient Information

Medgnosia is an educational study tool, not a medical device or a clinical record system. It is not intended for diagnosis, patient management, or treatment. Do not enter real patient records, identifiable health information, or confidential clinical material into the app.

11. Your Choices

You can choose whether to provide optional profile details, whether to use camera and notification features, and whether to export or share content. You can manage Android permissions in system settings and delete locally stored app content using the controls described in this policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page indicates when the current version was published. Please review this page periodically.
